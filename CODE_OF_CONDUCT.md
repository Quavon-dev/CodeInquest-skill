# Code of Conduct

CodeInquest welcomes technical disagreement, critical review, and evidence-based debate. Participation must remain professional and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Give review feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Be willing to revise claims when stronger evidence appears.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and perspectives.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or deliberate disruption.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is not misconduct. Strong criticism of an implementation, security claim, benchmark, architecture, or contribution is allowed when it remains focused on the work and is supported by reasoning or evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or moderate contributions and interactions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
